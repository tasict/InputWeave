# InputWeave brand assets

Every file here is generated by `apps/macos/scripts/make-brand-assets.swift` from one geometry; do not edit them by hand. To change them, change the script and run it from the repository root:

```sh
swift apps/macos/scripts/make-brand-assets.swift
```

## Concept

The brand's "seam": a laptop and a desktop screen side by side, and **the cursor is the needle**. A purple running stitch leaves the laptop's screen, passes through where the two screens meet, and leads to the tip of the cursor on the other screen. The stitch starts with a knot.

- The app icon (redesigned on 2026-10-23): the tile is the seam's purple, slightly lighter at the top (`#9D56E6`) and darker at the bottom (`#7A2FC8`); the screens are plain white without outlines, the stands pale purple (`#CBA9EE`), and the canvas dots 14 % white. The stitch and the knot keep the Thread color, drawn on the white screens. The tile has no baked-in shadow (neither the Dock nor the taskbar adds one to icons); the faint shadow under the screens scales with the size. The same icon works on light and dark backgrounds, so there is no dark version. Before the redesign the tile was the light gray Canvas color, which vanished in the Dock and on the installer's white pane, and its shadow was a fixed 1024 px value that turned into a haze at small sizes.
- The marks without a tile (`mark.svg`, `logo.svg`) use only the five brand color tokens: screens in Tile with Tile edge outlines and stands, the stitch in Thread, in a light and a dark version.
- The cursor stays the system cursor, black with a white outline.
- The smaller the size, the fewer the details:
  - 64 px: the stitch becomes a solid line, the cursor and the dots go, and the screens grow a little.
  - 32 px and below: only the two screens and a bolder stitch remain, with a margin of the tile's purple.

| `../../apps/macos/Installer/dmg/background.tiff` | The DMG window's background (600×400 pt, 1x and 2x): two screens meeting on the dotted canvas, with the seam; Finder places the app and the Applications shortcut on the two screens (layout in `layout.applescript` in the same folder). The menu bar icon is a template image the app draws at run time (`StatusIcon.swift`), matching the 32 px and smaller layer: two screens side by side and the stitch through the gap, without stands, cursor or dots. Each state's look is in `StatusIcon.swift`.

Screenshots of the app, including the comparison of the menu bar icon's five states ([`status-icon-2x.png`](../screenshots/status-icon-2x.png)), are made by `apps/macos/scripts/make-screenshots.sh` and kept in [`docs/screenshots/`](../screenshots/README.md).

## Word mark

The word mark uses the system font (SF Pro Display / Segoe UI Variable), with "Weave" in the seam color. The SVG holds text, not outlines, so where those fonts are missing another sans-serif font is used; when the look must be fixed (for print, say), convert the text to outlines in a design tool first.

## Windows

The notification area icons (`apps/windows/InputWeave/Assets/Tray`: an ICO for each of the five states, for light and dark taskbars, 16–64 px) are drawn by `apps/windows/scripts/MakeTrayIcons` from the same geometry as `StatusIcon.swift`; see `apps/windows/README.md`. The Windows app icon `apps/windows/InputWeave/Assets/InputWeave.ico` (used by the executable, the Start menu shortcut and Settings › Apps) is made by `apps/windows/scripts/MakeAppIcon` from the macOS app icon's PNGs above: 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96, 128 and 256 px, each scaled down from the smallest PNG not smaller than it, so it keeps that one's level of detail (32 px and below have only the screens and the bolder stitch). After the brand assets change, run it again on Windows; `AppIcon.ico`, `AppIcon.png` and `AppIcon256.png` for the window, first-run setup and notifications are written by `make-brand-assets.swift` directly and need nothing else.
