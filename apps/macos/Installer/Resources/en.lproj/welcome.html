One keyboard and mouse for every computer on your desk.

This installs InputWeave in the Applications folder, with the background service that starts when you log in and shares your keyboard and mouse.

When the installation finishes, InputWeave opens and walks you through setup, which asks for the permissions it needs. To update later, open the package of the new version; your pairings and settings are kept.

To remove InputWeave from this Mac instead, choose Remove InputWeave at the Installation Type step.
