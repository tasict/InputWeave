The InputWeave license

InputWeave is free and open source, and every feature works without a license. A license costs US$10 once and marks your copies as licensed.

Buy a license for US$10 Licensed

What you get

A Licensed label in place of “Trial”, in Settings and in About, on up to 5 computers. Nothing else changes: InputWeave works the same with or without a license.

Buying

Polar sells the license and handles the payment, taxes and receipt. After you pay, the license key is on the order page and in the email Polar sends you.

Adding the key

On any one of your computers, open Settings and choose License at the bottom of the sidebar, then Enter License Key. Then, on that computer’s License page, choose Apply License next to the other computers that should use it, up to 5 in all; each activates itself, and one that is offline does as soon as it connects. Paired computers never get the license by themselves. Any licensed computer can apply the license, and Revoke License frees a computer’s place again.

Five computers

Each computer that uses the license takes one of its 5 places. On a Mac or Linux computer, each user account that uses the license counts as one of the 5. A sixth computer stays in trial and still works in full. To move the license, revoke it on a computer you no longer use from any licensed computer, choose Remove License on that computer, or remove it in Polar’s customer portal.

Building it yourself

The source is under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later, so you can build and change InputWeave yourself. The license pays for the official builds and keeps the work going. It is not a condition for using them.

What the app sends

The app contacts Polar only when a license key is added or applied, when it revokes one, and to confirm it now and then. The privacy page lists what is sent.
