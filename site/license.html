The InputWeave license

InputWeave is free and open source, and every feature works without a license. A license costs US$10 once and marks your copies as licensed.

Buy a license for US$10 Licensed

What you get

A Licensed label in place of “Trial”, in Settings and in About, on up to 5 computers. Nothing else changes: InputWeave works the same with or without a license.

Buying

Polar sells the license and handles the payment, taxes and receipt. After you pay, the license key is on the order page and in the email Polar sends you.

Adding the key

On any one of your computers, open Settings and choose License at the bottom of the sidebar, then Enter License Key. The computers you’ve paired with it pick up the license by themselves, and so does a computer you pair later.

Five computers

Each computer that uses the license takes one of its 5 places. On a Mac or Linux computer, each user account that uses the license counts as one of the 5. A sixth computer stays in trial and still works in full. To move the license, choose Remove License on a computer you no longer use, or remove that computer in Polar’s customer portal.

Open Polar’s customer portal

Building it yourself

The source is under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later, so you can build and change InputWeave yourself. The license pays for the official builds and keeps the work going. It is not a condition for using them.

What the app sends

The app contacts Polar only when a license key is added, and to confirm it now and then. The privacy page lists what is sent.
